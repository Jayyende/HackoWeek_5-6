# Chain Rule

## Definition

The chain rule is used to differentiate composite functions.

Example:

y = (x² + 1)³

Let:

u = x² + 1

Then:

y = u³

dy/dx

= dy/du × du/dx

= 3u² × 2x

= 6x(x²+1)²

## Applications

- Backpropagation
- Deep Learning
- Neural Networks

## Conclusion

Chain rule is essential for training neural networks.
